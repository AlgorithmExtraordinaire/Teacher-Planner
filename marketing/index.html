Collaboration

Planning stops being a folder on someone's laptop

Departments teach as a team. The planning ought to work that way too — shared, reviewable, and never dependent on one person being reachable.

◫

Co-planned lessons

Two teachers can build the same scheme of work without emailing versions back and forth. Changes are attributed, so it is always clear who wrote what.

▤

Shared curriculum drive

Textbooks, teacher guides and module materials indexed by subject, grade and teaching role — filed once, found by everyone.

✓

Departmental approvals

Submit a plan, a head of department signs it off, and the decision is recorded. No more "I thought you had approved it".

❝

Inline feedback

Comment on the differentiation section rather than the whole document. Mentoring happens where the work is, not in a separate thread.

Analytics

See the drift while there is still term left to fix it

Most schools discover a struggling class at reporting time. Continuous tracking moves that discovery forward by weeks.

◗

Student progress trends

Standards-based mastery per learner, per subject, over time — so a slide shows as a slope, not a surprise.

≡

Curriculum pacing

Compare planned against actual module completion by class. Falling behind becomes visible in week three, not week ten.

◎

Quality cycles

Define a measure, track it across a term, and keep the evidence of whether the intervention actually moved it.

▦

Attendance analytics

Attendance beside attainment, because the two explain each other more often than either explains itself.

Governance

Compliance you can evidence, not just assert

When an inspector, a board, or a parent asks how a decision was made, the answer should take a minute to find.

§

Master policy index

Every policy in one register with an owner and a review date, so nothing quietly goes stale for three years.

⧉

Append-only audit trail

Approvals, role changes and configuration edits are written to a log with no update or delete path. Records cannot be quietly rewritten.

⚿

Authorisation workflows

Role-scoped sign-off with a genuine ceiling: only a platform owner can change who holds which role.

⛨

Row-level security

Permissions live in the database, not the interface. A hidden button is not a control; a policy is.
